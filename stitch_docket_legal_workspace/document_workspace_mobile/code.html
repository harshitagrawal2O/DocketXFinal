Writ Petition

S.R. v Union of India

IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

Original Jurisdiction

Writ Petition (Civil) No. 123 of 2024

The Petitioner, a distinguished advocate with over three decades of practice, brings this humble petition under Article 32 of the Constitution of India, seeking the enforcement of fundamental rights guaranteed under Articles 14, 19, and 21.

CLAUSE 1.1: STATEMENT OF FACTS

The matter pertains to the arbitrary exercise of executive power in the context of digital surveillance and the lack of a robust judicial oversight mechanism, which threatens the independence of the legal profession.

It is submitted that the impugned notification dated 15th January 2024 fails to satisfy the 'proportionality test' as established by this Hon'ble Court in K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India. The state's interest, while legitimate, cannot override the sacred privilege shared between a counsel and their client.

"The right to privacy is an integral part of the right to life and personal liberty... it is the core of the human personality."

— Para 42, Puttaswamy (2017)

Furthermore, the Petitioner highlights that several archival documents, previously thought to be under the protection of the Official Secrets Act, have now been declassified in adjacent jurisdictions, creating a legal vacuum that needs immediate adjudication.
