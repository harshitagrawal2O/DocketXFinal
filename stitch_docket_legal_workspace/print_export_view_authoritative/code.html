MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

Date: 24th October 2023

This MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of the date first above written, by and between VORTEX TECHNOLOGIES PVT LTD, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Bengaluru (hereinafter referred to as the "Client") and LUMINA CONSULTING GROUP (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider").

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1

“Services” shall mean the strategic consulting and technological integration services provided by the Service Provider as specifically described in Annexure A attached hereto.

1.2

“Intellectual Property” shall mean all patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, and other proprietary rights of any kind.

ARTICLE 2: SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1

The Service Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with the prevailing standards of the industry and the specific timelines set forth in each Statement of Work.

2.2

Any modification to the Scope of Services must be mutually agreed upon in writing by authorized representatives of both Parties through a formal Change Request Document.

ARTICLE 3: CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party acknowledges that in the course of performance of this Agreement, it may obtain "Confidential Information" from the other Party. The receiving Party shall use at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own information of like importance, but no less than reasonable care, to prevent disclosure of the same.
