Shareholders' Agreement — Project Phoenix

Section 4.2 Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, no Shareholder shall transfer, pledge, or otherwise encumber any of its Shares without the prior written consent of the Board of Directors, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, a Shareholder may transfer Shares to an Affiliate provided that such Affiliate executes a Joinder Agreement in the form prescribed in Exhibit B. For the purposes of this Section, "Affiliate" shall mean any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the transferring Shareholder.

The Board shall have a period of fourteen (14) business days to review any proposed transfer request. If the Board fails to respond within this window, the consent shall be deemed granted. This provision is intended to prevent administrative stagnation during high-velocity capital rounds.

Each Shareholder represents and warrants that it has the full legal right, power, and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder. The execution and delivery of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate or other action on the part of such Shareholder.
