Shareholders’ Agreement by and between Khaitan & Co. and Global Tech Ventures

Draft v4.2.1 Modified: 14 Oct 2023 Status: Reviewing

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them below:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to any Person, any other Person that, directly or indirectly, Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with, such Person;

"Applicable Law" means any statute, law, regulation, ordinance, rule, judgment, order, decree, by-law, approval from any Government Authority, directive, guideline, or policy;

"Board" means the board of directors of the Company as constituted from time to time in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

2. EFFECTIVE DATE

2.1 This Agreement shall come into force and effect on the date first written above (the "Effective Date") and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with Clause 12.

2.2 Upon the occurrence of the Effective Date, the Parties shall perform all their respective obligations under the Transaction Documents to which they are party.

3. GOVERNANCE AND MANAGEMENT

3.1 The Company shall be managed by the Board. Each Party shall exercise its voting rights and other powers of control available to it to ensure that the Board is constituted in accordance with this Clause 3.

3.2 The number of directors shall not be less than three (3) and not more than seven (7). For as long as Khaitan & Co. holds at least 15% of the Fully Diluted Share Capital, it shall have the right to nominate one (1) director to the Board.

NOTE: Review the quorum requirements for Board Meetings. Current draft requires at least one Nominee Director from both Parties to be present.

3.3 Any director nominated pursuant to this Clause 3 may be removed or replaced at any time by the Party which nominated such director by giving written notice to the Company.
